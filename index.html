A Little About Arpit

I am Arpit Kukadiya — well-mannered, family-oriented and focused on my career. I believe in keeping a healthy balance between my professional goals and my responsibilities towards the people I love.

I have completed my BCA and MCA, and I currently work as an Associate System Support Engineer at InfosenseGlobal in Raysan, Gandhinagar, where I handle system support and IT operations.

Honesty, simplicity and mutual respect guide the way I live and build relationships. I deeply value our traditions and cultural roots, while keeping a progressive outlook on life.

I enjoy spending time with my family, exploring new places and keeping up with developments in technology. To me, understanding, patience and mutual support are the foundation of a successful marriage.
